Hybrid architecture combining PatchTST, iTransformer, TimesNet and BiLSTM with physics-informed loss (MSE + Haversine + velocity + acceleration + endpoint). Trained on IBTrACS Western Pacific typhoon tracks. State-of-the-art 24h trajectory accuracy.
